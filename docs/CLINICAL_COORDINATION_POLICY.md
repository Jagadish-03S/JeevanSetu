# JEEVANSETU - Clinical & Regulatory Governance Policy

## 1. Statutory Context & Legal Standards

JEEVANSETU operates in strict adherence to the regulatory standards of the Republic of India:
- **Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 and Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945 (Part XII-B)**: Governs the collection, processing, testing, and storage of human whole blood and blood components.
- **National Blood Policy (2002)** & **National Blood Transfusion Council (NBTC)**: Enforces strictly voluntary, non-remunerated blood donation across all licensed blood banks.
- **Supreme Court of India Ruling (Common Cause v. Union of India, 1996)**: Complete prohibition of professional/commercial blood donors and commercial blood broker networks.
- **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023**: Strict confidentiality safeguards for health and personal identity data.

## 2. Core Operational Guardrails

### A. Non-Emergency Coordination Status
JEEVANSETU is **not** an emergency medical dispatch service, ambulance provider, or casualty resuscitation unit. In life-threatening emergencies:
- Attending healthcare workers or family members must dial **112** (National Emergency Response Support System) or **108** (Ambulance).
- Emergency O-negative uncrossmatched blood release protocols remain the clinical prerogative of the treating hospital's trauma and casualty resuscitation officers.

### B. Pathologist-Exclusive Decision Authority
- **Transfusion Suitability**: JEEVANSETU algorithms **never** declare a blood unit safe for transfusion.
- **Cross-Matching**: Major and minor laboratory cross-matching must be performed physically in the blood bank laboratory prior to issuing any unit.
- **Mandatory Disease Screening**: Every collected unit of whole blood or apheresis product must undergo laboratory testing for:
  1. Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV-1 and HIV-2)
  2. Hepatitis B Surface Antigen (HBsAg)
  3. Hepatitis C Virus (HCV)
  4. Syphilis (VDRL / TPHA)
  5. Malaria parasites

### C. Anti-Brokerage & Non-Remuneration Guarantee
- No user, hospital, or facilitator is permitted to request, offer, or receive financial remuneration, gifts, or monetary incentives for blood units or donor coordination.
- Any attempt to monetize blood requests results in immediate account termination and referral to state law enforcement authorities.
